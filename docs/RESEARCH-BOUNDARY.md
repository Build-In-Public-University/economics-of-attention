# Research boundary

## Established in this repository

- A conceptual model of finite observers selecting and compressing information before action.
- Definitions for action-relative meaning, attention cost, attention efficiency, heterogeneous attention, alpha as an unsocialized distinction, and semantic hysteresis.
- A claim ledger with falsifiers and evidence ceilings.
- A proposed relationship to Shannon, Hayek, Grossman–Stiglitz, Transformer attention, expertise, language, and collective intelligence.

## Not established

- That human attention literally implements Transformer attention.
- That Shannon information is semantic meaning.
- That markets are mathematically equivalent to multi-head attention.
- That the equations are empirically estimated or theoretically proven here.
- That attention diversity causes superior market, team, or institutional performance.
- That any cited historical author anticipated modern machine learning.
- That any alpha, language, or expert-blindness effect has been measured in this project.

## Evidence status vocabulary

- `theory`: proposed conceptual mechanism.
- `definition`: stipulated quantity or notation.
- `bounded_hypothesis`: testable claim whose scope is explicitly limited.
- `prospective`: proposed experiment or field extension.
- `empirical`: reserved for observed, source-receipted results.
- `formalized`: reserved for checked formal derivations, not notation alone.
- `rejected`: a tested claim contradicted under its stated contract.
- `unresolved`: required evidence or contract is missing.

## Immediate falsifier

The broad thesis is weakened if increasing information abundance does not produce a measurable need for selective discrimination under fixed processing resources, or if attention mechanisms' survival is unrelated to the value they create net of operating cost.
